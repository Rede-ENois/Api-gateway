# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Énóis exists so that people can work, create, talk and build reputation without being exploited.
Everyone participating in this repository — code, issues, reviews, discussions and stories — commits
to keeping an environment that respects everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion, sexual
orientation or socioeconomic status.

## What we expect

- **Respect in criticism.** Disagree with the code, not the person. Technical criticism is welcome
  and must be specific ("this loop is O(n²) because…"), never about who wrote it.
- **Context before conclusion.** Read the surrounding code, the history and the documentation before
  assuming something is wrong.
- **Patience with people starting out.** This project has its own conventions (they are in
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`) — explain, do not humiliate.
- **Discretion with other people's data.** Never publish personal data, credentials, tokens or
  screenshots of private conversations in an issue, PR, print or comment. Mock data in stories and
  tests is obviously fake.
- **Technical scepticism, not hype.** "The tool works" is not an argument; code that passed the tests
  is. A claim without evidence — human or AI-generated — is treated as a draft.
- **Documented error, not hidden error.** When your change breaks something, say what broke, for
  whom and how it was fixed. Hiding a regression is worse than causing it.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, stalking, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Disparaging comments about personal characteristics, or unsolicited sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate attacks on the platform, on real users or on third-party infrastructure — including
  "security tests" without prior authorization (see `SECURITY.md`).
- Using the project's tools to defraud, deceive or harm other people.
- **Dumping AI output without review.** Opening a PR, issue or comment with code, text or a diagnosis
  that the sender has not read and does not understand is polluting the shared space — and is
  treated as such, not as a contribution.
- **Fabricating evidence.** Saying a test passed without running it, inventing command output, citing
  an API that does not exist, or attributing someone else's work to yourself.

## Scope

This applies to every space of the project: repositories, issues, pull requests, reviews,
documentation, and whenever someone represents the project in public.

## How to report

Write to **suporte@enois.ia.br** with what happened, where, when and, if available, screenshots or
links. Reports are handled confidentially, and good-faith reporters are not penalized — even if the
investigation does not confirm the incident.

For **security flaws**, the channel is different: **seguranca@enois.ia.br**, as described in
`SECURITY.md`. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

## Consequences

Anyone who does not follow this code may receive anything from a warning to permanent removal from
the project's spaces, depending on severity and recurrence. Decisions are recorded, with reason and
responsible person, and may be reviewed on request.
